About

An Electronics and Computer Science engineer who builds with data.

I studied Electronics and Computer Science Engineering (ECS) at KIIT University, which taught me to think in signals, noise and systems. I still work that way. Whether it is a legal search engine, a credit risk model or a support-ticket triage bot, I start by asking what signal matters and how I will know if the system got it wrong.

My projects span AI and LLM apps, machine learning, analytics, finance and risk, product operations, full-stack web, and signals and simulation. Most of them come with a test suite, a written list of known limitations, and a failure analysis, because a model that looks good on a demo is only half the job.

I am looking for roles in AI engineering, data and analytics, product, or software development where I can ship real things and keep learning fast.
